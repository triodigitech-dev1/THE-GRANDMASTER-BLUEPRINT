# 01 — Personal Playing Profile

## Purpose

Establishes an honest, documented baseline of who you are as a chess player: strength, style, history, resources, constraints, and goals. It is the reference point against which all later progress is measured, and it is revisited at least annually.

## What to Store

- Player profile document (from the template): rating history, title status, playing style, strengths, weaknesses, time and resource constraints.
- Goal statements: long-term (multi-year), annual, and quarterly, each with a measurable criterion.
- Baseline assessment results: tactics test, endgame test, opening knowledge check, calculation test (cross-reference `03`).
- Historical game collection index (PGN databases live outside or in `05`; index them here).
- Health, schedule, and constraint notes affecting training (sleep, work, travel, budget).
- Annual profile snapshots, kept for comparison.

## Recommended Workflow

1. Complete `player-profile-template.md` in the first week; be specific and evidence-based.
2. Run baseline tests and record dates, conditions, and scores.
3. Write goals in measurable form (rating, performance, norm attempts, test scores, hours) and note *why* each matters.
4. Save a dated copy each year (`profile_YYYY.md`) instead of overwriting.
5. After each annual review (`07`), update strengths/weaknesses and goals using the evidence gathered.

## Expected Outputs

- A completed baseline profile.
- Clear, measurable goals with deadlines.
- A yearly snapshot that makes long-term change visible.

## Review Process

- Ask: are claims in the profile backed by data from `05`, `07`, `08`?
- Compare this year's snapshot with last year's. What changed, and does the evidence support why?
- Check that goals are realistic given current rating trend and available training hours; revise honestly.
- Distinguish knowledge, playing strength, coaching skill, and title progress in your self-assessment.

## Suggested Subfolders or Templates

Recommended: `baselines/` (test results by date), `snapshots/` (annual profiles), `goals/` (goal statements). Included template: `player-profile-template.md`.
