# 06 — Engine and Mentor Reviews

## Purpose

Manages external feedback. Engines and stronger human mentors provide different information: engines give concrete evaluation, mentors give explanation, prioritization, and judgment. The folder also enforces a disciplined way of using them so that they sharpen your thinking instead of replacing it.

## What to Store

- Engine analysis exports (engine name, version, hardware settings, depth or time) linked to specific games.
- Mentor session notes and recommendations.
- Lists of disagreements between your assessment and external assessments.
- Engine-use protocol and log of how you used engines.
- Comparison tables of engine vs. mentor advice where they differ.
- Questions to bring to the next mentor session.

## Recommended Workflow

1. Prepare for each mentor session: choose specific games, positions, and questions in advance.
2. Record advice using the mentor session template, including actions and deadlines.
3. Use engines *after* your own analysis; document settings so results are reproducible.
4. Note whether the engine's line was humanly usable; ask why the recommendation works.
5. Convert every disagreement into a training item or a diagnosis input.

## Expected Outputs

- A clean record of feedback received and actions taken.
- A measure of your evaluation accuracy relative to engines and mentors.
- A documented engine-use protocol that you follow.

## Review Process

- Track how often your unaided assessment matched the engine and the mentor.
- Check whether mentor recommendations were implemented and whether they helped.
- Watch for over-reliance: analysis conclusions that you cannot explain without the engine.
- Review the protocol quarterly.

## Suggested Subfolders or Templates

Recommended: `engine-reports/`, `mentor-sessions/`, `disagreement-log/`, `protocol.md`. Included template: `mentor-session-template.md`.
