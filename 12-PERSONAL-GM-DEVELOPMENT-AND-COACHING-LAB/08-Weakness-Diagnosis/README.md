# 08 — Weakness Diagnosis

## Purpose

The coaching-of-yourself centre. It converts scattered errors from games, tests, and feedback into evidence-based diagnoses with root causes, prescriptions, and follow-up tests. It is where you practise coaching skill on your own chess.

## What to Store

- Weakness ledger listing each identified issue, its evidence, priority, and status.
- Weakness diagnosis records (from the template).
- Error statistics aggregated from `05`, `03`, and `07`.
- Prescribed interventions and their results.
- Resolved-issue archive with proof of improvement.
- Unresolved or recurring issues and hypotheses under investigation.

## Recommended Workflow

1. Every month, aggregate error categories from `05` and `03` and rank them by frequency and cost (points lost).
2. For each top issue, write a diagnosis: symptom, evidence, suspected root cause, alternative causes.
3. Design a prescription with a measurable target and a deadline; add it to the plan in `02`.
4. Run the intervention, then re-measure using the same method as the baseline.
5. Close, revise, or escalate the issue (for example, seek mentor input in `06`).

## Expected Outputs

- A prioritized, up-to-date weakness ledger.
- Diagnoses backed by data.
- Documented outcomes for each intervention.

## Review Process

- Ask whether the diagnosis addresses cause or merely symptom (e.g., 'missed tactic' vs. 'rushed candidate search after long opening think').
- Verify every 'resolved' item with fresh evidence, not the feeling of understanding.
- Check for bias: are you avoiding categories that are uncomfortable to examine (psychology, time, preparation quality)?
- Seek an outside review of your diagnoses periodically.

## Suggested Subfolders or Templates

Recommended: `ledger.md`, `diagnoses/`, `interventions/`, `resolved/`. Included template: `weakness-diagnosis-template.md`.
