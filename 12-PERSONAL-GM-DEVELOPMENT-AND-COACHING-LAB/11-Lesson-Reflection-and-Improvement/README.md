# 11 — Lesson Reflection and Improvement

## Purpose

A structured process for evaluating your own teaching after each lesson and periodically across lessons. It converts experience into improved methods and prevents repeated instructional mistakes.

## What to Store

- Post-lesson reflections (from the template).
- Student feedback (structured forms or notes) where appropriate.
- Peer or mentor observation feedback.
- Teaching-method experiments: what was tried and results.
- Quarterly teaching reviews.
- A list of teaching skills to develop (explanation, questioning, pacing, feedback, motivation).

## Recommended Workflow

1. Within 24 hours of each lesson, complete a reflection: objectives met, evidence, what went well, what did not.
2. Compare the planned lesson (`09`) with what actually happened.
3. Log method experiments with a hypothesis and result.
4. Each quarter, aggregate the reflections to identify recurring patterns and set 1-3 teaching improvement goals.
5. Update lesson materials and feed the changes back to `09`.

## Expected Outputs

- A body of reflections that shows learning as a teacher.
- Revised lesson plans and methods.
- Quarterly teaching goals and evidence of progress.

## Review Process

- Check whether reflections cite evidence (student responses, results) instead of general impressions.
- Ask whether student outcomes in `10` improved after method changes.
- Look for recurring problems, such as talking too much, overloading, or weak assessment.
- Seek external observation periodically; self-perception of teaching is often biased.

## Suggested Subfolders or Templates

Recommended: `reflections/YYYY/`, `feedback/`, `experiments/`, `quarterly-reviews/`. Included template: `lesson-reflection-template.md`.
