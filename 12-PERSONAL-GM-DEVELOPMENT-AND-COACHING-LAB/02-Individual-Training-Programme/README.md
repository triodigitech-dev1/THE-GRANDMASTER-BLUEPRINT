# 02 — Individual Training Programme

## Purpose

Turns goals and diagnoses into a structured, periodized training plan and records what was actually done. Integrates study from other chapters of The Grandmaster Blueprint with the specific needs revealed by your games and diagnoses.

## What to Store

- Annual training plan and quarterly/monthly cycle plans, each with objectives and success criteria.
- Daily/weekly training logs (from the template), including time spent and quality of focus.
- Study plans mapping curriculum chapters to current needs.
- Tournament preparation and recovery plans.
- Cycle retrospectives: what was planned vs. done and what changed as a result.
- Reading and course lists with completion status.

## Recommended Workflow

1. At the start of each quarter, translate the top diagnoses from `08` into 2-4 training objectives with metrics.
2. Allocate weekly hours across categories: game analysis, calculation, openings, endgames, strategy, play, physical recovery.
3. Fill in the training log every session, including what you did, how well you concentrated, and what you learned.
4. Weekly: summarize actual vs. planned hours and key learnings.
5. End of cycle: write a retrospective and link to evidence in `05`, `07`, `08`.

## Expected Outputs

- A written plan for the current cycle.
- A complete, honest training log.
- Retrospectives that show what training actually influenced results.

## Review Process

- Compare planned and actual hours; identify why deviations occurred.
- Ask whether each activity had a stated purpose and measurable output.
- Check the balance: too much passive study (reading, videos) relative to active work (solving, analysis, play)?
- After a cycle, test whether the target weakness improved in the data. If not, revise the method, not only the effort.

## Suggested Subfolders or Templates

Recommended: `plans/YYYY/`, `logs/YYYY-MM/`, `retrospectives/`, `tournament-prep/`. Included template: `training-log-template.md`.
