# 12 — GM Title Requirements and Progress

## Purpose

Tracks the formal, external requirements for the FIDE Grandmaster title and your position relative to them. This is deliberately separate from knowledge, training volume, and coaching skill: a title is awarded for meeting FIDE's published conditions in rated competition.

## What to Store

- A summary of the current FIDE title requirements with the exact regulation references and the date you last verified them.
- Tracker of relevant elements: rating thresholds, norms, number and type of games, opponent conditions, and event eligibility.
- Event planning: candidate tournaments, entry requirements, norm opportunities, costs, and travel.
- Records of norm attempts, with cross-tables, opponent details, performance calculations, and official documents.
- Records of any titles already achieved, and intermediate titles if relevant.
- Correspondence with the national federation regarding titles and applications.

## Recommended Workflow

1. Read the current FIDE Handbook Title Regulations directly. Do not rely on memory or secondary summaries; regulations are revised.
2. Create a requirements checklist with the regulation citation and date checked.
3. Update the tracker after every rated event; include the rating list and the norm-relevant details of the event.
4. Plan events in advance by their eligibility for norms and rating, considering the timeline and budget.
5. Review honestly each quarter: compare the current level with the requirements and revise the timeline based on evidence (`07`).

## Expected Outputs

- A verified, dated checklist of formal requirements.
- A complete record of norm attempts and results.
- A realistic, evidence-based timeline that is revised when data warrants.

## Review Process

- Verify the regulations at least every six months and before any application.
- Compare your performance evidence (`07`) with what is required; state the gap plainly.
- Separate outcome from ability: a strong performance in the wrong event conditions may not count toward a norm.
- Remember that no training programme guarantees a title; keep options open and revise goals as needed.

## Suggested Subfolders or Templates

Recommended: `regulations/` (notes with citations and check dates), `norm-attempts/`, `event-planning/`, `documents/`. Included template: `title-pathway-tracker-template.md`.
