# 09 — Coaching Practice

## Purpose

Develops teaching capability as its own discipline. Strong players are not automatically good coaches; this folder builds skills in explaining, structuring lessons, assessing levels, giving feedback, and designing exercises.

## What to Store

- Lesson plans (from the template) organized by theme and student level.
- Teaching materials: exercise sets, handouts, model games, slide outlines, worksheets.
- Curriculum maps for different student levels and goals.
- Diagnostic tests and assessment tools for students.
- Notes on coaching methods, communication, and feedback techniques.
- Recordings or notes from practice teaching sessions (with consent) and observations of other coaches.

## Recommended Workflow

1. Begin with objectives: define what a student should be able to do after the lesson, in observable terms.
2. Write the plan using the lesson plan template; include an assessment method.
3. Practice by teaching study partners, giving mock lessons, or explaining material in writing.
4. Build reusable resource sets and note what works with which student level.
5. Feed real lesson experience into `11` and students' data into `10`.

## Expected Outputs

- A tested library of lesson plans and materials.
- Clear curriculum maps for at least a few student profiles.
- Demonstrated ability to explain the same idea at different levels.

## Review Process

- Check for alignment: does the assessment actually measure the stated objective?
- Have someone else review materials for clarity and accuracy.
- Note the distinction between your own understanding and the ability to transfer it.
- Update materials based on reflections in `11` and outcomes in `10`.

## Suggested Subfolders or Templates

Recommended: `lesson-plans/`, `materials/`, `curricula/`, `assessments/`, `observations/`. Included template: `lesson-plan-template.md`.
