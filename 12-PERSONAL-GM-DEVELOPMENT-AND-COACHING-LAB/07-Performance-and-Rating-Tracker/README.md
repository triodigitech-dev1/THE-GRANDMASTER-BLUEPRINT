# 07 — Performance and Rating Tracker

## Purpose

Provides objective measurement of playing strength through rated results, performance ratings, and process metrics. It is the counterweight to subjective impressions and the primary source for deciding whether training is working.

## What to Store

- Tournament results ledger (event, dates, time control, opponents' ratings, score, performance rating, rating change).
- Rating history by list and by platform, with source and date.
- Process metrics: average move quality, blunder rates, time-trouble frequency, opening scores.
- Monthly, quarterly, and annual performance reviews.
- Scheduled events and the purpose of each.
- Notes on external factors (illness, travel, format) that affect interpretation.

## Recommended Workflow

1. After every rated event, enter results the same day.
2. Record process metrics using the same method each time, and note any method change.
3. At month end, complete a performance review comparing the results with the plan and the baseline.
4. Compare results by color, opponent strength, time control, and opening.
5. Feed findings into `08` and next cycle's plan in `02`.

## Expected Outputs

- A complete, accurate results ledger.
- Regular reviews with data-based conclusions.
- Trend charts for rating and key metrics.

## Review Process

- Look at multi-month trends; do not react to single events.
- Distinguish variance from real change: consider sample size and opponent strength.
- Do not compare online rapid or blitz rating with classical strength without caution; keep them separate.
- State what the data does *not* show. Uncertainty is part of the analysis.

## Suggested Subfolders or Templates

Recommended: `results-ledger.csv` or spreadsheet, `reviews/monthly/`, `reviews/quarterly/`, `reviews/annual/`, `charts/`. Included template: `performance-review-template.md`.
